Delete your Sheepish account

This page is for deleting your account and data for the Sheepish app, made by Genesis 2 LLC.

Delete from inside the app

Open Sheepish, go to Profile, then Settings, and choose Delete account. Your account and your personal information are removed.

Delete without the app

Email support@playsheepish.com with the subject "Delete my account", from the email address on your account. You do not need the app installed. We will confirm it is you, then delete your account and personal information within 30 days.

What gets deleted

Your account, your sign-in email, your name and profile photo, your answers and messages, your voice recordings, and your preferences. Backups are cycled out within 30 days. We may keep records we are legally required to keep, such as purchase records for accounting.

One thing to know: some of your history belongs to the two of you together, so parts of it may stay visible to your partner after you leave, because it is theirs as well. If you want your shared history removed entirely, say so in your email and we will take care of it.

Your subscription is billed by Apple or Google, not by us, so deleting your account does not cancel it. Cancel it in your App Store or Google Play subscriptions. The support page has the exact steps.

The full detail of what we collect and keep is in our Privacy Policy.
